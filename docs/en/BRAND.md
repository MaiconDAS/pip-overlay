# Visual identity

[Português](../BRAND.md) · [English index](README.md)

Name: **PiP Overlay**. Brand line: **Your video. Your game. Your space.** The Portuguese banner uses “Seu vídeo. Seu jogo. Seu espaço.”; the app header uses “Seu vídeo por perto. Seu jogo em foco.”

Background `#0F141E`, surfaces `#19212E`, accent `#6CE7C4`, primary text `#F3F8FA`, secondary text `#9AA9BE`. Typeface: Segoe UI, falling back to Arial/sans-serif. The symbol combines a screen and floating video. Editable SVG assets do not use external fonts or scripts.

`docs/assets/logo.svg` is the vector mark; `banner.svg` is the repository header; `social-preview.png` is the 1280×640 sharing image; `workspace-016.png` is the current sanitized interface preview. `editor.png` and `workspace-015.png` are historical previews. Application ICO/PNG assets are generated by `scripts/Render-AppIcon.ps1`.

Use plain language and state compatibility limits. Do not promise zero cost, universal game support or endorsement by browser/game vendors.
